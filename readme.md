### Website Idea & Structure

The website should reflect a professional, reliable, and premium wholesale brand. The design should be clean, using colors that evoke freshness and quality (like white, gold/wheat, and deep green). It needs to clearly communicate their expertise, product range, and make it easy for B2B clients to contact them.

**Suggested Sitemap:**

1. Home
2. About Us
3. Our Products / Brands
4. Why Choose Us (B2B Focus)
5. Contact Us
