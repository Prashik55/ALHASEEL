Direct Sourcing. Decades of Wholesale Mastery.

Founded in Dubai in 2019, Karawan Al Aseel bridges the gap between global agricultural mills and commercial food operators across the United Arab Emirates.

Our Heritage

Built by Food Industry Veterans

While Karawan Al Aseel Foodstuff Trading LLC was formally established in 2019, our leadership team brings more than 30 cumulative years of dedicated food supply chain experience across the Middle East.

Having observed traditional supply models burdened with cascading brokers and unnecessary distribution layers, we set out to build a streamlined, transparent model: connecting commercial food producers directly with premier milling sources.

This direct model ensures our B2B partners — from neighborhood bakeries to large supermarket distributors — receive the highest grade grains with guaranteed freshness, verifiable laboratory parameters, and significant savings on their landed costs.

Our Mission

To eliminate unnecessary supply layers, delivering pure, unadulterated foodstuff with maximum operational reliability.

Our Slogan

"Treat yourself to healthier cooking" — the standard behind every metric ton we dispatch.
